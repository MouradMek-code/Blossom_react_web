Blossom – Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Blossom (the Blossom dating app on Google Play, package com.mouradmek.blossom, and the website blossom-date.com) is for adults aged 18 and older only. Blossom has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). It is strictly prohibited on Blossom, in every form.

What is prohibited

  • Any child sexual abuse material (CSAM): images, videos, drawings or text that sexualize a minor.
  • Grooming: contacting, befriending or manipulating a minor for sexual purposes.
  • Sextortion, and any request for sexual content from or about a minor.
  • Trafficking or the sexual exploitation of a minor in any way.
  • Any account belonging to, or pretending to be, a person under 18.

How we prevent it

  • Every member must confirm a date of birth showing they are 18 or older; sign-up is blocked otherwise.
  • Every account is confirmed with an email code, and every profile needs real photos.
  • Members can verify themselves with a selfie checked by our team, and we can ask any suspicious profile to verify.
  • Members can block anyone, and every report is reviewed by the Blossom team.

How to report a concern

  • In the app, without leaving it: on any profile, tap Report; or open Settings → Report a problem → Child safety concern. Visitors can use Report a problem or a safety concern at the bottom of the app's home screen.
  • On the website: blossom-date.com/report – no account needed.
  • By email: mourad.meknioui@gmail.com (subject "Child Safety Report").

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

What we do

  • Reports about child safety are reviewed first and acted on immediately.
  • Any account involved in CSAE is banned and its content removed at once.
  • We report child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and/or the relevant law enforcement authorities, and preserve what they need.
  • We cooperate fully with law enforcement investigations.

Compliance with the law

Blossom complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the obligation to report child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities.
